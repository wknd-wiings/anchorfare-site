Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

AnchorFare is a cruise deal tracker operated by MVP Forge LLC of Knoxville, Tennessee. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the AnchorFare website and app collect, what they don't, and how anything collected is used.

No account, no sign-in

Browsing AnchorFare — on the website or in the app — needs no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, or password just to look at cruise deals, and we do not sell anything on this site.

What the app collects

The AnchorFare app collects a small amount of anonymous technical information, used for analytics only — to understand which deals people look at and to keep the app running well:

  • Usage data — product interaction, such as which screens and deals are viewed.
  • Diagnostics — crash data and performance data, so problems can be found and fixed.

This data is not linked to your identity, is not used to track you across other apps or websites, and is never sold.

The website

Like most websites, AnchorFare's hosting may keep standard technical logs of visits (such as pages requested and general connection information) and may use ordinary cookies or similar basics needed for the site to work and to understand overall traffic. We do not build profiles of individual visitors, and we do not sell personal information — we don't collect the kind of personal information there would be to sell.

Prices and booking

The prices shown on AnchorFare come from the cruise lines' publicly available offers and are verified before they are featured. AnchorFare finds the fare; booking happens on the cruise lines' own websites (or with your own travel agent), and once you leave AnchorFare, that cruise line's own privacy policy and terms apply to anything you share there.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone. Full stop.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
